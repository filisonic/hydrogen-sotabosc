A refined linear scroll experience for Sotabosc featuring scattered 'Specimen Hotspots'.

**UX CORE CONCEPT:**
- Return to the **Linear Vertical Scroll** (Story 1.2).
- As the user scrolls vertically through the 6 layers (Sky to Mycelium), they encounter 'Specimen Nodes' scattered in the parallax environment.
- These nodes are the "littered products" - bioluminescent hotspots that reveal specimens on click.

**VISUAL LANGUAGE (From .stitch/DESIGN.md):**
- **Colors**: Vibrant HSL palettes.
- **Atmosphere**: Vertical parallax journey.
- **Hotspots**: Pulsing, glowing nodes integrated into the flora.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. A vertical cross-section showing layers transitioning from one to another.
2. In the 'Understory' layer, show 3-4 scattered 'Specimen Nodes' pinned to different depths.
3. One node is in a 'Hover' state, showing a glassmorphic quick-view with a product link.
