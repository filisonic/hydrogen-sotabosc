The life under the canopy

sotabosc (Catalan, n.): the undergrowth. Everything that grows beneath the tall trees, where most of a forest’s life actually happens.

Barcelona’s landmarks get the attention. Sotabosc is about the rest: the galleries, workshops, studios, roasters and rooms where the city’s creative life actually grows.
