Issue zero

From the editors

Every day the Sotabosc desk reads the city: openings, festivals, workshops, the small rooms where things start. Most of it ends up on screens in cafés, studios and coworking spaces, as Sotabosc TV. This is the same desk, on paper.

Issue zero follows the rule we offer every business in our network: most of the pages for the world around you, the rest for your own house. Here the house is us. Pages 3 to 9 are Barcelona in November. Pages 10 to 15 explain what Sotabosc is, and how a café, a gallery or a studio can have a channel and a magazine of its own.

We summarise, we credit, we link out. Nobody appears without saying yes.

— The Sotabosc desk
