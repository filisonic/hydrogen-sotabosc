Neighbours, not noise

A café’s guests are a gallery’s next visitors. A coworking space’s members are a bookshop’s best customers. Barcelona’s small places have always sent people to each other by word of mouth; the network makes that a habit.

Every host can invite neighbours onto its screen, and appear on theirs. Only places that both say yes are paired, and only for as long as they want. Nobody buys their way onto another host’s wall.

The magazine does the same on paper: each issue has a page for the street, and the places on it are chosen by an editor, not an auction.

For visitors, the city at sotabosc.world keeps a passport. Each place they visit adds a stamp from its kingdom, which is a small reason to walk one street further.

The more hosts there are in a neighbourhood, the richer each screen becomes: more local stories, more events, more reasons for a guest to stay a little longer and come back.

A café’s guests are a gallery’s next visitors. The network just makes it a habit.
